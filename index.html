LAYN is an independent audio and film production house based in Morocco.

Working across Africa and international markets, we carry projects from the first raw idea to the final structural delivery: music, recording, sound, film, and production management under one single, unified practice.

01 / Concept

LAYN grows out of a real, two-sided music practice: Moroccan classical music on one side, urban African music (rap, R&B) on the other. Around that sits expert work on music videos, scenarios, and highly developed productions.

Two musical worlds. Heritage and street, acoustic and digital. LAYN is the structural line drawn directly between them.

02 / Character

Our approach is clean, cinematic, minimal, precise, and human. The visual identity intentionally stays quiet so that the raw, creative energy of the work carries the underlying culture.

The Moroccan and African identity comes purely through the real people, places, sounds, light, architecture, and stories we produce—never through cosmetic decoration.
